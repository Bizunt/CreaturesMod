# Creatures Mod

A Minecraft Forge mod that adds four mythical creatures to the game.

## Creatures

### Allosaurus
- **HP:** 150
- **Behavior:** Large aggressive dinosaur found in plains biomes
- **Weapon:** Diamond Sword
- **Spawns:** In groups of up to 2

### Apple Cow
- **HP:** 10
- **Behavior:** Peaceful cow variant that drops apples
- **Spawns:** In groups of up to 4 on grass blocks

### Basilisk
- **HP:** 150
- **Behavior:** Dangerous creature found in underground/dark areas
- **Weapon:** Iron Sword
- **Spawns:** In groups of up to 2

### Ice Spider
- **HP:** 12
- **Behavior:** Fast spider in cold biomes that applies slowness on hit
- **Weapon:** Iron Sword
- **Spawns:** In groups of up to 3

## Installation

1. Download the latest release JAR file
2. Place it in your `mods` folder
3. Launch Minecraft with Forge 1.20.1

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.2.0 or higher
